## Vocabulary (PEDA009)

Six flags, each read back against the lesson it came from:

| class | count | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — ligature artifact: the book **has** the word, mangled | 3 | false positive |
| B — morphological: the root **is** in the book | 2 | false positive |
| C — genuinely absent from the lesson | 1 | correct flag |

Class A is the reversed lam-alef defect in the PDF's text layer: the book
spells `سلامت` as `سالمت` (twice, lesson 3) and `خلاقیت` as `خاّلقیت`
(lesson 8). The model wrote the words correctly and was flagged for it.

Class B is prefix matching meeting Persian morphology: `چیزی` against the
book's `چیزهای`, and `کرده` against `کردی` (lesson 1). Same three-letter root,
different inflection.

Class C is a single word, `قرار` in lesson 1, which shares not even two leading
letters with anything in that lesson. The flag is correct by the rule. But
`قرار گرفتن` is a light-verb construction and `قرار` carries almost no meaning
on its own, so what leaked is grammar, not knowledge.

**No general knowledge, and no technical term above the book's level, entered
any of the 79 objectives.**

## Observability

78 of 79 observable. The one exception, lesson 13:

```
statement : توضیح دهد که آهنرباها گاهی یکدیگر را جذب می‌کنند.
verb      : 'توضیh دهد'      ← Persian ح replaced by a Latin h
```

A character-level corruption in the model's output, not a pipeline fault: the
statement itself is sound and its quotation matched exactly. The closed verb
lexicon caught it precisely because matching is a lookup rather than a
judgement — a free-form "does this look like a verb?" check would have accepted
a string one character away from a valid one. `PEDA001` fired, and the
objective is in the review queue rather than in the output as if it were fine.

## Known limits, carried forward deliberately

- **Reversed lam-alef in the source PDF.** `کلاس` arrives as `کالس` in ten of
  fourteen lessons. The broken spelling collides with real words (`سال`,
  `حال`), so `text/persian.py` reports these for human review instead of
  guessing at a repair. Cost: three of six wording flags.
- **Prefix matching cannot see a Persian prefix.** `متفاوت` in the book does
  not vouch for `تفاوت` in a claim.
- **`min_word_length = 4` hides three-letter words.** `دما` and `ذوب` cannot
  be detected in any lesson. Harmless for grade 1, where neither appears;
  it is configurable per book and should be revisited before a higher grade.

## Reproducing these numbers

The artifacts are not in the repository — they are run outputs, and the book is
not ours to redistribute. Each lesson directory holds `objective-prompt.txt`,
`objective-raw.json`, `objective-verified.json`, `objective-validation.json`,
`objective-run-summary.json` and `objective-review.md`; 85 files across the 14
lessons. Regenerating them needs the source PDF, the L0 extraction, and a model.
